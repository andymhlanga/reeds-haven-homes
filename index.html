WELCOME TO REEDS HAVEN

More than a house.
A sense of belonging.

Reeds Haven Homes is an Edmonton-area supportive living organization with a focus on welcoming residential environments for adults. Our vision is a home where every person feels safe, respected, heard, and connected to the wider community.

Our mission is to nurture belonging and everyday independence through compassionate, person-centred relationships. We listen first, respect individual preferences, celebrate strengths, and work toward a meaningful quality of life.

OUR FOCUS

Support that starts with understanding.

Our approach to adult supportive living is guided by individual choice, consistent relationships, daily wellbeing, and meaningful community participation. We believe a home should support each resident’s preferences, goals, and sense of belonging.
